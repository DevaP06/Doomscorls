# Doomboard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-19_

Doomboard counts how many Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts you scroll each day.

## What stays on your device
- Your daily reel count and watch time. Doomboard never reads captions, usernames, video titles, comments or any other page content. It only looks at the page address (to tell one reel from the next) and whether a video is playing.

## What is shared, and only if you join the leaderboard
Joining is optional and off until you choose it. If you join, Doomboard sends to our server (hosted on Supabase):
- the nickname you chose,
- a random anonymous ID generated on your device,
- your daily totals: number of reels and seconds watched.

Nothing else is sent. Doomboard does not collect your email, name, account or browsing history. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as part of running the service.

## Your control
- **Leave:** "leave & delete my data" in the popup permanently deletes your nickname and all your scores from our server and stops all sharing.
- **Rename:** change your nickname any time.
- **Uninstall:** removes everything stored on your device. Use "leave" first if you also want your server data deleted.
- We periodically delete profiles that have been inactive for a long time.

## Public information
Your nickname and today's totals are visible to other Doomboard users on the leaderboard.

## Sharing and selling
We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than our hosting provider (Supabase), which stores it on our behalf.

## Children
Doomboard is not directed at children under 13.
